Rules

  1. Flip the coin once.
  2. Roll the die once.
  3. Select the matching profile.
  4. The profile is locked for the entire round.
  5. Play the available tee closest to the printed tee yardage.
  6. Use the club whose printed total distance is closest to the required total distance.
  7. Attempt to hit the required total distance with that assigned club.
  8. When two clubs are equally close, use the shorter-hitting club.
  9. If Driver is assigned off the tee, choose Driver off the deck or next-closest available fairway wood, hybrid, or iron.
  10. The required distance does not change under the Driver exception.
  11. Putter is always allowed from the putting surface.
  12. No rerolls unless the round is reset.
