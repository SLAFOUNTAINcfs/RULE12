How to Play

Choose Your Character

Tap Roll My Golfer. One gender and age archetype is rolled, then three unique rarity versions of that same golfer are revealed.

Choose a rarity. That character's distances, target difficulty, rewards, putting quest, and short-game quest are locked for the round.

Distance Quests

Under NEXT SHOT, choose the listed yardage closest to the shot you need. Hit or miss is judged by final total distance.

Around the Green

Use the calculator within 25 yards of the front edge. It recommends a club, landing zone, and predicted rollout.

Putting and Holes

Choose a starting hole. Recording putts completes the hole and advances to the next sequential hole. Partial-round putting bonuses are prorated as you play.

Score

Gross strokes include full shots, chips, putts, and penalties. Earned distance, chip, and putting rewards are subtracted for the Roll Play Score.
