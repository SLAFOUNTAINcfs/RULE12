How to Play

  1. Tap Randomize My Round, then choose one of the three rarity cards shown.
  2. Your selected profile is locked for the full 18-hole round.
  3. Review your active card and play the tee closest to the listed tee yardage.
  4. In NEXT SHOT, open Target Yardage and select the listed yardage closest to the shot you need.
  5. The app assigns the matching club and shows the Target, Target Zone, and Reward.
  6. Hit the shot, then report the outcome with one button: Hit Target Zone or Missed Target Zone.
  7. Scoring is based on where the ball finishes (total distance), not where it first lands.
  8. If you hit the target zone, you earn the displayed stroke reward for your locked rarity.
  9. If you miss the target zone, no stroke reduction is awarded for that shot.
  10. Tap Next Shot to continue, or Undo to remove only the most recent recorded shot.
  11. Use Optional details only when needed to adjust Hole Number, Shot Number, or add a note.
  12. If Driver is assigned off the tee, you may use Driver off the deck or a next-closest available wood, hybrid, or iron.
  13. The target yardage and target zone do not change under the Driver option.
  14. Putter is always allowed from the putting surface.
  15. No rerolls during an active round unless you reset the round.
