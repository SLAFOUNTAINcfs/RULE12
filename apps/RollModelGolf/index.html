Roll Model Golf

Choose your role model, they choose your target and club.

RNG gives you three cards to choose from. You choose the risk to reward.

Common cards are the most frequently appearing cards in the game. They are low risk and low reward.

Rare cards are less frequently appearing cards in the game. They are medium risk and medium reward.

Epic cards are the least frequently appearing cards in the game. They are high risk and high reward.

Legendary cards are the rarest cards in the game. They are very high risk and very high reward.

The printed numbers are total distances, not carry. You are scored based on where the ball finishes.

How to Play

Start Your Round

Tap Randomize My Round and choose one of the three rarity cards shown.

Your selected profile is locked for the full 18-hole round.

Play from the available tee closest to the yardage listed on your card.

You cannot reroll during an active round unless you reset the round.

Play a Normal Shot

Under NEXT SHOT, select the listed target yardage closest to the distance you need.

The app shows the assigned club, target distance, target zone, and stroke reward.

After hitting the shot, select Hit Target Zone or Missed Target Zone.

Judge the shot by where the ball finishes, not where it first lands.

Driver Option

When Driver is assigned away from the tee, you may hit Driver off the deck or use the next-closest wood, hybrid, or iron shown on the active card. The target distance and target zone do not change.

Around the Green

Use Around the Green when the ball is within 25 yards of the front edge.

The calculator considers 5-Iron through Lob Wedge and recommends the best club for the required carry and rollout.

Enter distance to hole and distance to front edge. The app recommends club, landing zone, and predicted rollout.

Chip targets: Common (full flagstick), Rare (half flagstick), Epic (three player foot lengths), Legendary (one player foot length).

A holed chip counts as a successful chip target.

Putting

When on the putting surface, select Putting and record total putts for that hole.

Select 0 for a hole-out from off the green, or 1-7 for exact putts.

Putting totals track across all 18 holes and award a round-end rarity bonus when qualified.

Final Score

Final Roll Model score may include distance-target reductions, chip-target reductions, and a putting bonus.

Only successful targets remove strokes from gross score.

HOLES AND SCORING

Choose your starting hole before recording your first shot.

Every shot is recorded against the current hole.

Recording putts completes the hole and moves the game to the next sequential hole.

After Hole 18, the sequence wraps to Hole 1 when the round began from a shotgun starting hole.

Gross score equals full shots, chip shots, putts, and penalties.

Target and chip rewards are subtracted from the gross score.

The putting bonus updates live as completed holes are recorded and reaches its full value when all 18 holes are complete.
